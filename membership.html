Membership

Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club would be proud to earn your support and membership. We offer annual plans in the following options:

Family Membership: $160.00
This membership allows the initiator, their partner and their children under the age of 22 to access the range for a full year.

Individual Membership: $140.00
This membership allows for the initiator access to the range for a full year.

Archery Membership: $160.00
This membership allows for the initiator access to the gun range as well as the archery range for a full year.

If you prefer to visit ISC as a guest the following options are available:
Daily Non-Member fees:
$30/day - Adult
$10/day - Active Military w/I.D.
$5/day - Under 18 w/ adult

ISC does not charge any additional fees to access the range. No ‘lane fees’, no time restrictions beyond regular business hours. Consider reviewing the Etiquette and Range Rules

If you'd like to join us as a Member or visit our range on a day pass you can expedite your check in process by filling out the waiver below. Membership begins once you arrive at the range for your first visit.
