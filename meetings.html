Monthly Meetings

Issaquah Sportsmen's Club meets on the 1st Monday of the month (except when it is a holiday, the meeting takes place on the 2nd Monday of the month) All meetings take place at the ISC Club House.
The Meeting starts at 7:00PM.

The Cascade Mountain Men meetings take place on the Tuesday before the third Sunday of each Month at 8PM. Meetings take place at the ISC club house.

The Club House is also used for Boy Scouts' meetings, Bow Hunter Education courses, basic Hunter Education Courses and occasionally special events for club members.

The Club House can be rented for meetings and parties. If you are interested in renting the Club House, please contact Tom Mechler at 425-255-0922 or email him at tmechler2@gmail.com.
