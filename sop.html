SOP and By-laws
Chapter 1: Preamble

Whereas range activities are open to the public, a few events that occur are: Hunter Education, General Marksmanship, Personal Protection Training, Basic Marksmanship Training, Boy Scouts of America Rifle Merit Badge Classes and other events. Whereas Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club is under certain liabilities for public safety during open operations, the Board of Directors of the Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club has determined the instituting of a set of Standard Range Operating Procedures to be desirable.

Therefore, the Executive Committee of the Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club has adopted this Standard Range Operating Procedure to establish a minimum standard requirement for any user of the range in King County, Washington by the Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club.

This Standard Range Operating Procedure will be reviewed for updates on a date set by the Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club Executive Committee and becomes effective thirty days after the Officers affix their signatures.

Chapter 4: Safety Plan
Emergency Procedures
  1. Take charge of the situation.
  2. Cease firing, unload, and stay in place commands are issued.
  3. Evaluate the situation.
  4. Determine the severity of the injury.
  5. Designate helpers, if necessary
  6. Render Aid.
Training and equipment
  1. First aid training & CPR are required for paid RSOs.
  2. The first aid kit is currently located inside the range office. The trauma kit is located on the firing line adjacent to the exit door.
  3. Determine level of care (Do you treat on site or call emergency services).
  4. Call for help. Phone numbers are posted in the office.
Provide emergency dispatcher necessary information:
  1. Location (Specify clubhouse, range, etc)
  2. Phone number of where the call is being made.
  3. Your name
  4. Describe the situation that instigated the call
  5. List the number of person(s) injured
  6. Describe the condition of the injured
  7. Location of person who will direct help to the scene.
  8. Direct help to the location:
    1. Position one or more persons to direct the emergency vehicle
    2. On the main road to the entrance of the facility
    3. On the road to the range
    4. Outside the building.
  9. Take notes (Reports)
  10. Take notes on the times actions were taken
  11. Get statements from witness(es) and quarantine from each other.
  12. Complete the required SOP’s form
  13. Notify authorities if necessary
If there is a local emergency not connected with the range, all personnel and patrons shall be kept on the premise until such time that an all-clear is given.
Dealing with the Media:
  1. All information about any incident should be given to the Secretary of Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club or his/her designee.
  2. Remind all persons involved that they are not to talk to the media.
  3. All questions from the media are to be directed to the President of Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club or his/her designee.
Chapter 5: Range Operations
1. Range Capabilities.
The range governed by this Standard Operating Procedure is composed of two (2) separate shooting sub-ranges referred to as follows:
3.1 The Archery Range is open to any bow and arrows equipped with field or target tips.
3.1.1 No firearms are permitted on this range.
3.1.2 Broadhead arrow tips are only allowed in the sand pit.
3.2 The Firearm Range is open to any center-fire rifle, any center-fire handgun legal for Washington big Game hunting, rim fire rifle or pistol, or any muzzle loading rifle or pistol.
3.2.1 The Firearm Range is open to Shotguns, 10 gauge or smaller using only #7 ½ shot or smaller firing at stationary targets only.
