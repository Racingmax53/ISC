We now offer a youth air rifle program! If you are just curious about the shooting sport or want to compete in air rifle and air pistol competitions, then look no further. That is exactly what we aim to do.

Kids Basic Marksmanship
This is a fun way to get started for kids. Basic aiming and shooting skills are taught with fun, interactive targets.

Air Rifle/Pistol 10-meter competition
Once you get a little more skilled and are looking for a challenge, we offer our 10-meter air rifle & pistol shooting. We use official competition airguns and targets and will have scheduled competitive tournaments with prizes!

All you have to do to participate, is become a member of the Issaquah Sportsmen's Club, which also gives you many other perks! Once you are a member, come to a safety orientation before training and you're ready to go!
