Heading East on I-90

Take Exit #17, the 2nd Issaquah exit.
Go Right at the bottom of the exit on to Front St.
Go straight thru the Gilman Blvd, Front St. light.
Take a left on E. Sunset Way.
Come to the next stop light,
Turn right on to 2nd Ave.
Turn left on S.E. Evans St.
At the turn you will see a sign saying "Sportsmen's Club".
Half way up the hill, S.E. Evans will become a gravel road.
At the end of the road 'T' make a right.
Turn left into our driveway.

OR

Take Exit #18 (E. Sunset/Highlands Dr.) Exit - The 3rd Issaquah Exit.
Take the right split on off-ramp - towards E. Sunset Way.
At the light, turn right. You will now be on E. Sunset Way.
Proceed to the next stop light.
Turn left on to 2nd Ave.
Turn left on to S.E. Evans St.
At the turn you will see a sign saying "Sportsmen's Club".
Half way up the hill, S.E. Evans will become a gravel road.
At the end of the road 'T' make a right.
Turn left into our driveway.

Heading West on I-90

Take Exit #18 (E. Sunset Way/Highlands Dr.) Exit
Stay to the left till you get to the stop light.
Make a left towards E. Sunset Way.
Continue down the hill till you get to a stop light.
Turn left on to 2nd Ave.
Turn left on to S.E. Evans St.
At the turn you will see a sign saying "Sportsmen's Club".
Half way up the hill, S.E. Evans will become a gravel road.
At the end of the road 'T' make a right.
Turn left into our driveway.
