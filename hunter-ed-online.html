Hunter Education at Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club

Issaquah Sportsmen’s Club is proud to host Washington Department of Fish & Wildlife (WDFW) Hunter Education courses. These classes, taught by certified WDFW instructors, cover firearm safety, hunting ethics, wildlife conservation, and outdoor skills. While the courses are held at our facilities, all registration and scheduling are managed directly through WDFW. To view available classes and sign up, please visit the WDFW Hunter Education website.
